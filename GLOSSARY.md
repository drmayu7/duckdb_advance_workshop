# Glossary

Terms used across the DuckDB Workshop Series 2 materials.

## Series 1

The first DuckDB workshop, run about three months before Series 2. It covered installing DuckDB, using the DuckDB UI, and core SQL: CREATE TABLE, SELECT, joins, GROUP BY, HAVING, ORDER BY, LIMIT, SAMPLE, CTEs, subqueries and filtering. Series 2 assumes this as known material and does not re-teach it, apart from a short Series 1 refresher.

## Series 1 refresher

The short session that opens Series 2, right after the setup check: a one-page recap of Series 1 SQL followed by a Warm-up Challenge on the Scenario data. It reminds Participants of what they learned three months earlier and gets them using the DuckDB UI again. It is not a re-teach of Series 1.

## Warm-up Challenge

The Challenge in the Series 1 refresher. It uses only Series 1 SQL, on clean Scenario tables, and has a Self-check and a Reveal like any other Challenge.

## Series 2

This workshop ("Bengkel Aplikasi SQL and Data Wrangling Menggunakan DuckDB, Siri 2 Tahun 2026"), run 11–13 November 2026 for KKM staff. It focuses on intermediate and advanced DuckDB, with data wrangling as the main theme.

## Programme

The official day-by-day schedule of sessions (the "Tentatif Program", Lampiran 1). Series 2 keeps the original 3-day frame and the Capstone, but module names and content may be reshaped.

## Module

A themed block of sessions in the Programme, such as "Data Import/Export & File Formats". A Module can run over more than one time slot.

## Extension track

A self-paced, card-driven Challenge built around one DuckDB extension (statistics, name matching or spatial). After a short facilitator tour of all the tracks, each Participant picks two.
_Avoid_: rotation, station (these imply parallel floor helpers, which Series 2 doesn't have)

## Capstone

The team project in which Participants build an end-to-end pipeline, from raw MyHDW-like data to health insights using DuckDB SQL, and then present it for peer review.

## Participant

A workshop attendee from a KKM health informatics centre: doctors, statisticians, MOH IT staff and medical record officers working on the Malaysian Health Data Warehouse (MyHDW) project.
_Avoid_: student, trainee

## MyHDW

The Malaysian Health Data Warehouse, the real national system that Participants work on. Workshop materials never contain real MyHDW data.

## MyHDW-like dataset

The synthetic dataset built for the workshop. It mirrors the shape of MyHDW data (patients, admissions, diagnoses, facilities, states) and contains no real patient records. It may be combined with Malaysian open data for realism.
_Avoid_: MyHDW data (when referring to workshop data)

## Offline bundle

The workshop data and DuckDB extensions packaged in advance and given to Participants on a USB drive or by download, so the hands-on sessions do not depend on the venue's internet connection.

## Scenario

The single running health story that every Challenge and the Capstone are set in, so each Module builds on the previous one instead of starting from unrelated examples.

## Challenge

A hands-on task given to Participants after a short demo, followed by a reveal of the solution. Each Challenge has a Core tier and a Stretch tier, and comes with a Self-check.

## Core tier

The part of a Challenge that every Participant is expected to finish, whatever their role or SQL fluency.

## Stretch tier

Optional extra parts of a Challenge for Participants who finish the Core tier early.

## Self-check

A way for a Participant to confirm their own Challenge answer is correct without the facilitator, essential because Series 2 has one facilitator and no floor helpers.
It compares the Participant's result with the Challenge's expected result: an exact match means done.

## Checkpoint

The expected result of a Challenge, which a Participant who didn't finish loads in place of their own answer, so that later Challenges in the Scenario still work. The same expected result is what the Self-check compares against.

## Reveal

The facilitator's walkthrough of a Challenge's solution after the time box ends. The solution is in the repo from the start, kept apart from the Challenge so Participants can choose not to see it early.
