# Capstone peer-review rubric

> **Synthetic data, not real hospital performance.** Patients and admissions are made up. The hospital names are real, so no number here says anything about a real hospital.

Each Participant scores the **two other teams** on a score slip (`capstone/score_slip.md`), giving each of the four criteria a score from **1 to 4**. There is no middle score: decide whether the team is closer to 1 or to 4. The rubric is formative. It is there to give each team useful feedback, and teams are not ranked.

| Criterion | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Answer to the Director** | No clear number | A number, but not for 2021, or with no comparison | A clear 2021 number, compared with 2020 but not explained | A clear 2021 number, with the change from 2020 explained in one sentence |
| **Data-quality story** | No issue shown | An issue named, but not how it was found or fixed | Symptom, how it was found and the fix, but no number before and after | Symptom → how found → fix → number before and after |
| **Pipeline** | Steps missing or run by hand | Most steps in `pipeline.sql`, some by hand | Runs top to bottom, but the stages or the fix are hard to follow | Clear stages that run top to bottom, with the fix commented |
| **Communication and caveats** | Over time, no caveat | Over time, or no caveat | Within 7 min with a caveat, but questions not handled well | Within 7 min, a sensible caveat, questions handled |

The slip also asks for **one thing this team did well**. Write something specific: the facilitator reads one out for each team at 2.45.

**What peers don't score:** whether the number is correct. Over lunch, the facilitator compares each team's summary with the true 2021 figures and reports the result separately at 2.45. It is never added to the peer scores.
